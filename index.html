Privacy Policy for Nivas KirayaBook

Last Updated & Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Welcome to Nivas KirayaBook ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal and rental ledger data is handled securely and transparently.

🔒 Our Core Commitment: Nivas KirayaBook is designed as a private, local-first digital ledger for property owners and landlords. We do NOT sell, broker, or rent your data to advertisers or third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Nivas KirayaBook collects and processes only the information necessary to provide rental management, electricity calculation, and reminder services:

  • Landlord / Owner Information: Name, contact phone number, property/building name, and owner UPI ID (used solely to generate direct payment links for tenants).
  • Tenant & Room Records: Room/flat numbers, tenant names, contact phone numbers, fixed monthly rent amounts, move-in dates, and electricity sub-meter readings.
  • Financial Ledger & Expenses: Payment history, rent receipts generated, and property maintenance expenses recorded by the owner.
  • Authentication Information: When signing in via Google, we receive basic profile info (display name, email address, and profile photo) from Google Authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information entered in the app is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To compute accurate electricity sub-meter bills and monthly grand totals.
  • To generate pre-formatted WhatsApp reminders containing your direct UPI link for hassle-free rent collection.
  • To generate legally compliant, printable HRA rent receipts for tenants upon payment.
  • To calculate monthly income, pending collections, and expense reports.
  • To sync your records securely across multiple devices when Google Cloud Sync is enabled.

3. Data Storage & Cloud Security

Local-First Storage: By default, your data is stored directly in your device's browser memory (localStorage and Persistent Storage API). It remains on your phone or computer even when you are offline.

Google Cloud Firestore: When you connect your Google Account via Firebase Cloud Sync, your ledger is encrypted in transit and securely stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Mumbai asia-south1 region). Access is strictly restricted to your authenticated Google account through Firebase Security Rules.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with trusted industry services solely for core functionality:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (Google Sign-In) and cloud database synchronization.
  • WhatsApp: When you trigger a rent reminder, the app opens standard WhatsApp web links (wa.me) on your device. The app does not read your private WhatsApp messages.
  • UPI Apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm): Direct deep-links (upi://pay) are created with your UPI ID. All transactions occur directly between the tenant's bank and your bank. We never hold or process your money.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your records:

  • Export & Backup: You can download a full JSON backup of all your rooms, tenants, and receipts at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Data Deletion: You can reset or permanently delete all your stored data directly via the "Reset / Clear All Data" feature in the Settings menu.
  • Account Disconnect: You can sign out or disconnect your Google Cloud account with one click.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or data privacy requests regarding Nivas KirayaBook, please contact us at:
